# Check for 0.8.43: group/ungroup and linked objects (two DIFFERENT accounts)

Status of this version: Planner-tested only. Nothing counts as working until you have seen it on both devices.

1. A puts three notes on a Canvas and groups two of them. Within about a minute B sees the frame and the two notes inside it. The Cloud screen on A does NOT say "not shared yet".
2. B moves the group (drag the frame). A sees the frame and both notes move together.
3. B ungroups it. A sees the two notes free again and the empty frame still there.
4. A adds a linked object (a live link to a task) and a snapshot card. B sees both. A changes the link's view (tree, fishbone, mind); B sees the change.
5. A groups two notes while B deletes one of them at the same moment. Nothing breaks; the Cloud screen may say "needs your review" for one change.
6. A deletes a frame that has notes in it. B sees the frame gone and the notes free (as before).

Write down for each step: as described, not as described, or unclear, and what you saw.
